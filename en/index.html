About

I was born in Denizli in July 1986. That is where I first discovered both photography and theatre, during my high school years. Then I moved to Kayseri, and while studying Astronomy and Space Sciences at Erciyes University I never left the stage.

I started explaining astronomy in 2005 and have not stopped since: in schools, in planetariums, at TÜBİTAK's observing festivals, and even on mountain roads in the middle of the night, looking up with fellow hikers. This path also led me to international work. At the Space Generation Advisory Council (SGAC) I served first as Türkiye's representative and then as Middle East Regional Coordinator. Since 2024 I have been the International Astronomical Union's National Outreach Coordinator for Türkiye. Asteroit Kâşifi, where I go asteroid hunting with students, is one of my favourite stops along the way.

These days I live in Antalya. I teach astronomy and English at Antalya Sınav Koleji, and in the summers I teach astrophysics to high school students in the CTY program at Johns Hopkins University. Since 2020 I have also worked as a freelance science writer and editor, and every issue of All About Space Türkiye and How It Works Türkiye carries my work.

I haven't stopped being a student either. I am continuing my master's degree in Space Sciences and Technologies at Akdeniz University, and studying for an associate degree in media and communication at Anadolu University.

The rest of my time goes to a rock wall, a trail or the saddle of a bike. I write more than I read, and the blog I started in 2007 is still going. My newest project is Tides in Times, where short English stories accompany my photographs.

    Life

    Rock climbing and bouldering

    Bouldering at the gym, and real rock whenever I get the chance. Finally solving a route is a joy of its own.

    Trekking

    I am a trekking leader certified by the Turkish Mountaineering Federation. On night hikes we look up at the sky together.

    Outdoors

    I camp, paddle a canoe and swim in the sea whenever I can. Any excuse to escape the city lights will do; a tent and a dark sky are more than enough.

    Cycling

    Part of my life for nearly thirty years, and for many of them my most loyal way of getting around. When I ride, my action camera usually comes along.

    Photography

    I shoot digital, analog and, more and more, on my phone. Some of the stories on @tidesintimes begin with shots from my pocket.

    Theatre

    I started in high school in Denizli and carried on at university in Kayseri. I plan to return to the stage soon.

    Film, series and games

    Everything I watch is logged on Trakt. If I find a free evening, I'm usually playing something on Steam.

    Travel

    I have seen 40 countries so far, and astronomy opened the door to most of them. In Türkiye I have visited 77 of the 81 provinces, and I hope to reach the last four soon.
